# Design Systems

How visual and interaction rules become reusable systems that humans and AI agents can apply consistently.

## Research focus
Design tokens; typography roles; spacing systems; semantic colors; responsive tokens; component states; variants; composition primitives; motion tokens; accessibility contracts; theming; visual regression; documentation for agents.

## AI-agent question
A design system for agents must provide enough constraint to maintain coherence without forcing every page into the same generic composition.

Research both:
- invariants: what must remain consistent
- degrees of freedom: where creativity is encouraged
